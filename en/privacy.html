Only what you type into the contact form: name, company, phone, email, service type and your message. This site uses no tracking cookies and no active third-party analytics. For one purpose only: answering your inquiry and preparing quotations. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes. If you subscribe to panel notifications, the subscription is used only for FARADAY ENERGY S.R.L. operational notices.

Your inquiry reaches the company email (faradayenergycfc@gmail.com) and a Supabase cloud database. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by writing to faradayenergycfc@gmail.com.
